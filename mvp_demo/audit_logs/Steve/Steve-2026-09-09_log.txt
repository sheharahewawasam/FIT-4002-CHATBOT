The investment strategies for the Powell Superannuation Fund, as outlined in **Powell - SMSF Investment Strategy 2020.pdf**, include the following:

### **Key Investment Strategies**
1. **Diversification Across Asset Classes**  
   - The Fund aims to invest across multiple asset classes (cash, fixed interest, shares, property, and alternatives) to balance risk and return.  
   - Strategic asset allocation targets are set to maintain long-term growth while managing risk (see **Asset allocation range** table).

2. **Risk Management and Volatility Tolerance**  
   - Members are prepared for moderate to high volatility in returns.  
   - The Trustee assesses risks, including liquidity, diversification, and potential liability coverage, in line with **Section 52B and 52C of the SIS Act** and **Regulation 4.09**.

3. **Liquidity and Expense Coverage**  
   - Ensure sufficient liquidity to meet expenses and obligations as they arise.  
   - Maintain flexibility to adjust allocations if cash reserves temporarily exceed maximum limits (e.g., due to contributions or asset sales).

4. **Return Objectives**  
   - Achieve investment returns **exceeding CPI by at least 5% annually** over a rolling 5-year period.  
   - Generate total returns through a combination of income and capital growth, aligned with medium-to-long-term inflation-beating goals.

5. **Regulatory Compliance**  
   - All investments must comply with **superannuation legislation** and the Fund’s Trust Deed.  
   - Regular reviews of the strategy are mandated (at least annually).

6. **Insurance Coverage**  
   - No personal insurance is required at present, based on members’ financial positions and risk profiles.

### **Asset Allocation Targets**  
The Trustee maintains a strategic allocation framework (see **Asset allocation range** table):  
- **Cash**: 2–30%  
- **Fixed Interest**: 28–40%  
- **Australian Shares**: 24–80%  
- **International Shares**: 31–50%  
- **Property**: 0–20%  
- **Alternative Assets**: 8–20%  

These ranges allow flexibility to adapt to market conditions while preserving capital and enhancing returns.  

### **Review and Governance**  
- The strategy is reviewed **annually** and updated as needed.  
- Trustees prioritize balancing members’ risk appetite with return expectations.
