What is the basis for supervision of the SIS act 

Chatbot responded with:
The basis for supervision under the SIS Act is outlined in **Section 4(2) of SIS Act -1.pdf**. It states that the supervision applies because these funds and trusts are subject to regulation under the Commonwealth’s powers with respect to corporations or pensions (e.g., because the trustee is a corporation). In return, the supervised funds and trusts may become eligible for **concessional taxation treatment**. 

This forms the foundational rationale for regulatory oversight under the Act.
