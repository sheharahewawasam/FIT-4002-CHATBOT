The deed ensures the fund remains a complying superannuation fund through the following provisions:  

- **Legal Interpretation**: The deed is interpreted to ensure compliance with the Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act (SISA), ATO Guidelines, and other relevant regulations (Section 22(a) of deed.pdf).  
- **Adherence to Laws**: Trustees must always comply with superannuation laws and other relevant legislation (Section 22(b) of deed.pdf).  
- **Concessional Tax Treatment**: The fund must be operated to qualify for concessional tax rates under the Income Tax Assessment Act (Section 22(c) of deed.pdf).  
- **Trustee Obligations**: Trustees agree to act honestly, exercise skill and diligence, and manage the fund in the best interests of members (Section 24(a)-(c) of deed.pdf).  
- **Separation of Assets**: Funds must be kept separate from personal assets of trustees (Section 24(d) of deed.pdf).  
- **Investment Restrictions**: The fund is prohibited from engaging in non-prudent investment strategies that conflict with its purpose of providing retirement benefits (Section 6 and 7 of deed.pdf).  
- **Administrative Compliance**: The fund is administered by Superannuation Warehouse Australia Pty Ltd, which handles financial statements, tax agent duties, and auditing (Section 25 of deed.pdf).  

These measures align the fund’s operations with the requirements of a "Complying Superannuation Fund" under SISA.
