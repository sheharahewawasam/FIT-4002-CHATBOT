What is the basis for supervision of the SIS act 

Chatbot responded with:
The basis for supervision under the SIS Act is outlined in **Section 4(2) of SIS Act -1.pdf**. It states that the supervision is based on the principle that certain superannuation funds, trusts, and approved deposit funds are subject to regulation under the Commonwealth’s powers regarding corporations or pensions. This applies, for example, when the trustee is a corporation. In exchange for this supervision, these entities may become eligible for **concessional taxation treatment**. 

The Act explicitly excludes regulation of other superannuation industry entities not covered under this framework.
